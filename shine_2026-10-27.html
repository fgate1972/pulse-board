Tuesday 27 October 2026, 18:30 Dallas · the book as set, grade v2 · shadow, allow-list empty · go-live target Monday 2 November · claude.ai copy: https://claude.ai/artifact/7Cev1beYoDh66dCnWqFVX8
B+ gets one gate, A− gets none. A grade-B+ position is not taken when the 4-hour Fisher transform, read in the direction of the trade, already sits in the top third of its last 200 closed 4-hour bars — the move is late on the 4-hour. This makes B+ cleaner (fewer trades, more per trade, half the drawdown on that stream) but it is not a money-maker: it costs $30 a week on the 29-week test, and it would have skipped none of this week’s nine B+ trades. It is coded and running in the 21 shadows since 18:28.

What is coded and running

The rule. At the fill, for a B+ position on a symbol where B+ is on: compute the Fisher transform (period 9, the toolbox formula) on the closed 4-hour bars; rank the last value against the previous 199 (0 = lowest, 1 = highest); for a sell use 1 − rank. If that number is 0.67 or higher, skip. A+, A and A− are untouched. B− and C were never taken.
Where it lives. state\book_rules.json → "shine" (delete that block and the gate is off everywhere) · shine.py · tier_now.shine_skip · live_orders.tier_mult (logs SHINE …) · tools\board_set.py (skip reason “late on the 4-hour (shine)”) · tape_panel.py (chart calls).
Proven. The live function reproduces the study read on 966 of 966 B+ positions (max difference 0.000000) and the live 1,200-bar window matches the 3,000-bar study on all 124 positions of the last four weeks. How the gate was found (the DNA pass, 296 reads tried)

Every read evaluated alone on the A− (750) and B+ (966) positions of the 29-week record: 16 toolbox lenses (SDMI, RSI, CCI, Fisher, KAMA, TSI, CMO, TRIX, UO, WaveTrend, Donchian, StochRSI, Aroon, EMA stack, STC, QT) on M15 / M30 / H1 / H4, each as “oriented low third” and “oriented high third”; PulseMatrix2 paint with / against / grey; Galaxy oscillator with / against. Closed bars only. Each read was picked on the first 66% of the positions by time and scored on the last 34%; the bar: the skipped side earns at least 0.15 R less than the kept side in both parts, with at least 30 trades on each side in both parts.

B+: 15 reads pass, and the ten reads that ranked best in the early part score +0.23 R on the later part — the selection generalizes. Every one of the 15 says the same thing: the move is already stretched with the trade on a higher frame (Fisher H4, UO H4, CCI H4, StochRSI H4, STC H4, WaveTrend H4, paint H4 with, TRIX H1, STACK H1, TSI H1, Donchian H1, KAMA M15, QT M15; and Aroon / CMO M15 low third). The best by the early part alone is the 4-hour Fisher.

A−: 10 reads pass the bar, which is what chance produces on 148 tries, and the ten reads that ranked best in the early part score −0.10 R on the later part. Nothing holds forward. A− stays as it is.
